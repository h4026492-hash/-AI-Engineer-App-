# Refunds and Billing Policy

Lumen Analytics bills monthly in advance. Your billing date is the day of the
month you first subscribed.

## Standard refunds

You can request a refund within 30 days of your first payment for a full
refund, no questions asked. After the 30-day window, payments are
non-refundable.

Pro-rated refunds are not offered on monthly plans. If you cancel mid-cycle,
you keep access until the end of the period you already paid for, and no
further charges are made.

## Annual plans

Annual plans may be refunded in full within 14 days of purchase. After 14 days,
you may cancel at any time to prevent renewal, but the remaining term is not
refunded.

## How to request

Email billing@lumen.example with your account email and invoice number.
Refunds are processed within 5 business days and appear on your card statement
within 10 business days, depending on your issuer.

## Failed payments

If a payment fails, we retry on day 1, day 3, and day 7. Your workspace moves
to read-only mode on day 7 if payment still has not succeeded, and is archived
after 30 days of non-payment. Archived workspaces can be restored by paying
the outstanding balance.
